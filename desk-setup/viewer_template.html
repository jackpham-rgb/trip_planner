    Fit and setup numbers

    To build or buy

    • Riser, 18 mm ply: top 600 x 230, two sides 230 x 62, back rail 564 x 25 (all mm). Glue and screw, felt pads. Leaves a 564 x 212 x 62 mm keyboard garage.
    • Printed laptop feet, two 230 x 20 x 10 mm bars, so air reaches the Dell's bottom intake in the cubby.
    • Grounded US power strip with a 1.5-2 m cord to replace the LiOA. The 5 m LiOA cord has no ground and leaves about 3.7 m coiled in the basket.
    • Inline cord switch for the lamp. Its own switch sits on the plug, which ends up in the basket.
    • Footrest about 10 cm high (DIY), because a 30 in desk puts the seat above your knee height.

    Still assumed

    • Desktop height 30.0 in. A tape from floor to desktop top would confirm it.
    • Basket width 10.5 in from the top-down photo; the tape read 6 in.
    • Cable lengths: monitor DP and AC 1.5 m, brick DC 1.8 m, brick AC 1.0 m.
    • Laptop + monitor mode needs about 17 cm free to the left of the desk for the USB-C bend.
    • Lamp arm lengths and the LiOA body size are measured off photos.
